# Walmart Department Performance Analysis

## Objective
Identify the highest-performing departments using historical Walmart sales data.

## Key Findings
- Department 92 recorded the highest total sales at approximately $483.94 million.
- Department 95 ranked second at approximately $449.32 million.
- Departments 92 and 95 generated approximately $933.26 million combined.
- Department 92 had average weekly sales of $75,204.87.

## Business Recommendation
Prioritize high-performing departments for detailed demand forecasting and investigate their sales patterns during holiday periods.

## Limitation
Department identifiers do not reveal product categories without additional mapping information.
