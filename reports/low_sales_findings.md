# Walmart Low Sales Analysis

## Objective
Identify the five lowest-selling weeks across Walmart stores.

## Key Findings
- January 28, 2011, recorded the lowest weekly sales at $39.60 million.
- January 27, 2012, ranked second at $39.83 million.
- Four of the five lowest-selling weeks occurred in January.
- The lowest weekly sales were less than half of the peak weekly sales.

## Business Recommendation
Investigate January demand patterns when developing forecasts and inventory planning strategies.

## Limitation
The observed low-sales weeks do not establish why demand decreased.
