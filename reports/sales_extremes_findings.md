# Walmart Peak Sales Analysis

## Objective
Identify the five highest-selling weeks across Walmart stores.

## Key Findings
- December 24, 2010, recorded the highest weekly sales at $80.93 million.
- December 23, 2011, ranked second at $77.00 million.
- All five peak weeks occurred in November or December.

## Business Recommendation
Investigate November and December demand patterns when developing weekly sales forecasts and inventory planning recommendations.

## Limitation
The observed sales peaks suggest seasonal patterns but do not establish their cause.
