# Walmart Store Performance Analysis

## Objective
Identify the highest-performing Walmart stores using historical weekly sales data.

## Key Findings
- Store 20 recorded the highest total sales at approximately $301.40 million.
- Store 4 ranked second at approximately $299.54 million.
- Store 20 had average weekly sales of $29,508.30.
- Store 39 ranked tenth among the top 10 stores, with total sales of approximately $207.45 million.

## Business Recommendation
Investigate the sales patterns and department contributions of high-performing stores to identify opportunities for improved forecasting and inventory planning.
