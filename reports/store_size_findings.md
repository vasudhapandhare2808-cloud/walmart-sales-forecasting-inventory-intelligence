# Walmart Store Size Analysis

## Objective
Explore the relationship between store size and sales performance.

## Key Findings
- Store 13 was the largest store among the 15 records examined.
- Store 20 generated the highest total sales among these stores at approximately $301.40 million.
- Store 20 generated higher sales than Store 13 despite having a smaller recorded size.
- Store size alone does not explain the observed sales differences.

## Business Recommendation
Consider store size alongside store type, department mix, and historical sales when building forecasting models.

## Limitation
This analysis examined only the 15 largest stores, so it cannot establish the overall relationship between store size and sales.
