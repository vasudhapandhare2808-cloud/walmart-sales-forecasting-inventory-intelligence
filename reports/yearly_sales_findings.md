# Walmart Yearly Sales Analysis

## Objective
Evaluate annual sales performance using historical weekly sales data.

## Key Findings
- Walmart recorded approximately $2.29 billion in sales during the 48 recorded weeks of 2010.
- Sales increased to approximately $2.45 billion during 2011.
- Average weekly sales declined from $16,270.28 in 2010 to $15,954.07 in 2011.
- The 2012 dataset contains only 43 recorded weeks.

## Business Recommendation
Use weekly sales trends and time-based forecasting to evaluate changing demand. Compare equivalent periods when assessing annual performance.

## Limitation
2012 is a partial-year dataset, so annual totals are not directly comparable.
